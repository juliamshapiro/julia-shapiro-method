The Julia Shapiro Method

The Julia Shapiro Method is designed to help those experiencing relationship turmoil decide whether or not they should call it quits. The following Steps serve as a powerful tool, refined over the years, to help The Participant gain insights into their Current Romantic Relationship.

⚠Warning

The Method is not to be used lightly. The Julia Shapiro Method is a dangerous tool. Only complete The Steps when you truly feel ready to witness The Results.

Step 1 of 4

Miss Match's Mind Map

Create a Mind Map with at least 15 branches of anything that comes to mind when you think about the person you are romantically entwined with and your relationship to Said Person. Feel free to be abstract and free associate. This exercise works best when you are nonjudgmental with yourself, allowing any thoughts that arise to come through without resistance.

The Previous Partnership Pendulum

When choosing a partner, people commonly swing between two extremes of personality types. A negative experience on one side of the spectrum can push them toward seeking its opposite in the future.

Take a moment to consider your past relationships and identify your Prominent Partnership Pattern: are two of your Previous Partners on opposite ends of your Romantic Spectrum? What qualities do they have that differ? Feel free to enter in your own response.
